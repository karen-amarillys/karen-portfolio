Background & Context

RevoStore, a fictional e-commerce company, experienced a 13.6% decline in profit in the last quarter:contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}. This analysis was designed to identify the key root causes behind this decline and propose a structured action plan to achieve a 10% profit increase within the next quarter. The project followed a SMART-based business problem framework and included hypothesis prioritization, metric definition, and actionable recommendations to guide management decision-making.

Approach, Method & Strategy

Using the MECE and SMART frameworks, the problem was structured around five potential cost and revenue drivers: inefficient campaigns, increased discounts, increased fulfillment cost, material price hike, and delayed payments. Hypotheses were ranked based on impact and urgency, with the two most critical being increased discounts and rising material prices:contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.

  • Tools: Excel, Google Sheets
  • Frameworks: SMART, MECE, RCA
  • Metrics: Profit Margin Trends, Sales Uplift vs. Discount Depth, Conversion Rate
  • Scope: Campaign performance and cost structure over the last 3 months

Results & Key Insights

  • Profit Decline: Overall profit decreased by 13.6% due to increased material costs and excessive discounting.
  • Top Manufacturer Analysis: “Other” manufacturers contributed the most to sales but only ranked second in profit, capturing 10.57% profit margin:contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}.
  • Discount Inefficiency: High discount depth yielded minimal sales uplift, reducing overall margin efficiency.
  • Fulfillment & Material Costs: Both costs rose sharply due to logistics inflation and exchange rate impact on imported goods.

Recommendations

  • Margin Review: Re-evaluate the “Other” manufacturers’ pricing and profit margin structure to recover lost profitability.
  • Discount Optimization: Reduce the number or depth of discounts and focus on campaigns with proven conversion effectiveness.
  • Campaign Targeting: Refine audience segmentation to improve campaign ROI and reduce wasted ad spend.
  • Cost Control: Renegotiate supplier contracts or source alternative materials to offset import price increases.
