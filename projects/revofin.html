RevoFin — Portfolio Risk & Cohort Analysis

SQL-Based Risk Analytics | Yearly Analytical Report

SQL Risk Metrics Cohort Analysis Borrower Profiling

Background & Context

RevoFin is modeled as a lending/fintech portfolio. This project summarizes portfolio scale and repayment risk using core metrics, then evaluates repayment quality over time using cohort analysis to highlight where deterioration may occur and what borrower traits are associated with higher risk.

Approach, Method & Strategy

  • Defined and reported portfolio KPIs: OS (Outstanding), ENR (Exposure/earning-related), and TKB30 (repayment quality indicator).
  • Computed cohort metrics by issue year to compare repayment quality trends across vintages.
  • Profiled higher-risk cohorts using loan attributes (purpose, interest rate) and borrower attributes (income, employment, home ownership).

Results & Key Insights

  • Portfolio scale: 176,087 customers with total OS ≈ 28.01B and ENR ≈ 28.01B.
  • Portfolio health: TKB30 = 98.11% (only ~1.89% past due beyond 30 days), indicating overall strong repayment quality.
  • Cohort trend: Repayment quality improves in newer cohorts; the weakest cohort shows structural financial weaknesses.

Recommendations

  • Strengthen screening: Enhance asset-based screening (e.g., home ownership) and tighten approval thresholds for higher-risk profiles.
  • Early warning: Implement monitoring for high-interest borrowers and cohorts showing weakening repayment signals.
  • Risk-based pricing + controls: Maintain pricing by risk, but pair with stricter underwriting standards to protect repayment quality.
