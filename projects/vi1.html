Background & Context

OCA Indonesia is a Telkom Indonesia subsidiary providing omnichannel messaging solutions (WhatsApp, Email, SMS, and Call) for enterprise clients. This project analyzed historical messaging performance to understand user engagement patterns and channel effectiveness.

Approach, Method & Strategy

  • Integrated performance data across 4 messaging channels into a unified dataset.
  • Built an end-to-end Tableau dashboard for monitoring volume, delivery, and engagement metrics.
  • Analyzed time-based trends (weekday vs weekend, hourly patterns).

Results & Key Insights

  • WhatsApp consistently delivered the highest engagement rates.
  • Email showed high delivery volume but lower open consistency.
  • Engagement peaked during weekday business hours.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize WhatsApp for time-sensitive and high-impact campaigns.
  • Optimize Email usage for informational or long-form communication.
  • Schedule campaigns based on observed peak engagement windows.
