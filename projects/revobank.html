Customer Segmentation and Credit Risk Evaluation | RevoU Full-Stack Data Analytics

Python Google Colab RFM Analysis Clustering

Background & Context

At RevoBank Indonesia, I analyzed six months of credit card transaction and client data to uncover spending trends and customer segments that drive usage. The project evaluated profitability through a 1.5% transaction margin and adjusted for losses from fraudulent activity, helping identify key factors affecting income and user engagement.

Approach, Method & Strategy

Two datasets — card data and user data — were cleaned and merged using Python (Google Colab). Data preprocessing involved type correction, handling missing values, formating wrong data types, as well as removing duplicates and cards that have 0 credit limit and expired cards. Exploratory analysis then measured fraud rates, credit utilization, DTI ratio, and age-credit relationships. Segmentation used an RFM model (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) to profile customer engagement and identify high-value groups. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

  • Tools: Python (pandas), Google Colab
  • Analysis: EDA, RFM Segmentation
  • Scope: 10,000+ credit card users; performance, usage, and risk behaviors

Results & Key Insights

  • Average fraud rate: 0.22% — well within the safe industry threshold (below 1%).
  • DTI and credit score correlation: higher DTI aligns with lower credit scores, increasing risk exposure.
  • Age vs. Credit Limit: clients aged 20–50 hold the highest credit limits and engagement rates; above 60 shows decline.
  • Segmentation Results:
    • Champions (1,080) — highly profitable, frequent users.
    • Loyal Customers (904) — consistent contributors to stable revenue.
    • Potential Loyalists (805) — emerging customers showing promising growth.
    • About to Sleep (1,851) — large dormant segment needing re-engagement. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Recommendations

  • Credit Risk: Set stricter credit limits and monitoring for high-DTI users. Introduce financial education programs and low-interest repayment plans to mitigate default risk.
  • Customer Engagement: Offer exclusive perks and premium benefits for Champions and Loyal Customers to maintain satisfaction and prevent churn.
  • Retention: Implement win-back campaigns for “About to Sleep” and “At Risk” groups, emphasizing cashback or loyalty programs.
  • Growth: Nurture Potential Loyalists through tiered rewards, targeted promotions, and personalized upgrades.
