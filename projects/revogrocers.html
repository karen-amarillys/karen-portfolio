RevoGrocers — SQL Sales Performance Analysis

SQL Case Study | RevoU Full-Stack Data Analytics

SQL BigQuery Kaggle Dataset Data Joins Window Functions

Background & Context

RevoGrocers is a fictional retail company created for a SQL-based analysis project under the RevoU Full-Stack Data Analytics program. The objective of this project was to explore the relationship between product categories, revenue, customer engagement, and repeat purchase behavior using structured queries. All analysis was conducted using data sourced from Kaggle’s Grocery Sales dataset.

Approach, Method & Strategy

SQL queries were written and executed on BigQuery to analyze nine distinct business questions, including category-level revenue, repeat purchase rate, and top customer spending patterns. The process utilized advanced SQL concepts such as aggregate functions, CTEs, joins, and window functions to generate multi-layered insights from transactional data.

  • Tools: SQL, BigQuery, Kaggle dataset
  • Analysis: Category sales, customer segmentation, repeat purchase rate, top spender analysis
  • Scope: Over 6,000,000 transactions across 11 grocery categories

Results & Key Insights

  • Top Revenue Drivers: Confections (12.87%), Meat (11.39%), and Poultry (10.17%) contributed the most to total revenue.
  • Revenue–Quantity Correlation: Most categories showed a strong correlation between total units sold and revenue, except for Produce and Seafood which likely benefited from discounts or low pricing strategies.
  • Customer Engagement: A positive relationship between revenue and number of unique customers, though not perfectly linear.
  • Price Sensitivity: Slight negative correlation between average price and number of unique customers — higher-priced items attracted fewer buyers.
  • Repeat Purchase Rate: Extremely high overall (≥90%), with Confections and Meat achieving a perfect 100% rate.

Recommendations

  • Focus on Confections & Meat: These categories show strong loyalty and revenue contribution — ideal for upselling or premium line extensions.
  • Revitalize Low-Converting Segments: Review pricing and promotional strategies for Produce and Seafood to improve sales efficiency.
  • Customer Loyalty Strategy: Create loyalty programs targeting high repeat-purchase categories to sustain engagement.
  • Optimize Pricing Strategy: Perform elasticity testing on high-priced categories like Dairy and Grain to balance volume and profit.
  • Data Integration: Combine sales and customer demographics for deeper insight into shopper behavior and regional trends.
