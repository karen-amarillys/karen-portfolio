Background & Context

RevoU Next is modeled as a B2B SaaS business. This project evaluates the end-to-end sales funnel to identify where qualified leads drop off and what improvements could increase deal wins and revenue efficiency.

Approach, Method & Strategy

  • Calculated key funnel KPIs (bounce rate, session duration, cart abandonment, final conversion).
  • Mapped drop-offs across funnel stages to pinpoint mid-to-late funnel bottlenecks.
  • Segmented leads into customer clusters (Micro, SMB, Mid-Large) and compared stage-by-stage conversion.
  • Proposed a 90-day roadmap: quick wins → cluster strategy → long-term funnel measurement.

Results & Key Insights

  • Top-of-funnel health: Bounce rate 14.6% and average session duration 73s indicate relevant traffic.
  • Main friction: Cart abandonment at 35.23%, concentrated around Discussion → Offer Sent and Offer Sent → Quotation Received.
  • Overall outcome: Final conversion rate 26.5% (strong for B2B SaaS) with completion rate aligned to conversion.
  • Cluster signal: Conversion differs by company size, supporting tailored follow-up and proposal strategies.

Recommendations

  • Fix mid-late funnel: Standardize pricing/proposal format and set follow-up SLA (24–48 hours).
  • Automate follow-ups: Use CRM reminders (1–3–7 day cadence) after offers are sent.
  • Cluster strategy: Micro = simpler package; SMB = ROI calculator + objection handling; Mid-Large = dedicated account handling + customized proposals.
  • Measurement: Launch a monthly funnel dashboard + lead scoring to prioritize high-value leads.
