Background & Context

The RevoFinance project focuses on helping individuals monitor personal expenses, evaluate budget utilization, and identify spending trends. The dashboard was developed using real-world-style datasets to simulate everyday financial tracking, showing how data visualization supports smarter financial decisions and proactive budgeting.

Approach, Method & Strategy

Three datasets — Personal_Expenses, Merchant_Master, and Annual_Budget — were joined using inner joins based on merchant_id and category keys to ensure each expense was mapped correctly to both merchant details and its corresponding budget category. Tableau was used to visualize metrics such as total expenses, spending per category, and monthly trends.

  • Tools: Tableau, Google Sheets
  • Analysis: Expense tracking, budget utilization, trend analysis, and distribution insights
  • Scope: 1,000+ personal transactions across multiple spending categories

Results & Key Insights

  • Total Expense: Snapshot of overall spending, highlighting higher spending per transaction, indicating fewer but larger purchases.
  • Spend per Category: Utilities and dining showed the highest proportions, while online services are rising and need control.
  • Budget Utilization (%): Revealed overspending risks in certain categories — requiring adjustments to allocations.
  • Spending Trend: Monthly line chart showed seasonal surges (holidays, year-end), useful for predictive budgeting.
  • Transaction Distribution: Histogram indicated spending was habitual and mid-range — predictable, not sporadic.
  • Top Merchants: Bakso Pak Kumis, PLN Loket Jakarta, and Bioskop Nusantara — consistent spending drivers.

Recommendations

  • Budget Rebalancing: Shift underused funds toward overspent categories for a more balanced allocation.
  • Expense Awareness: Set monthly spending caps and alerts to prevent habitual overspending.
  • Category Control: Closely monitor utilities and online services to prevent repetitive high expenses.
  • Predictive Planning: Anticipate high-spending periods (holiday peaks) and allocate buffers in advance.
  • Merchant Analysis: Evaluate top merchants for potential subscription optimization or loyalty benefits.
