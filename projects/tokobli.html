Background & Context

The TokoBli Campaign Evaluation project aims to compare three major twin-date sales events — 10/10, 11/11, and 12/12 — to determine which campaign strategy yielded the most effective results. Using data provided for educational analysis, the study focuses on identifying the most successful campaign based on revenue generation, budget efficiency, customer engagement, and number of products sold.

Approach, Method & Strategy

The datasets included campaign-level records for total revenue, products sold, customer count, and discount budgets. Exploratory analysis compared performance across the three periods using ratio metrics such as Revenue/Budget and Revenue per Customer. Descriptive statistics and A/B testing were used to validate campaign efficiency and design differences.

  • Tools: Google Sheets, Excel
  • Analysis: Descriptive statistics, ratio analysis, A/B testing
  • Scope: Campaigns 10/10, 11/11, and 12/12

Results & Key Insights

  • Highest Revenue: 11/11 campaign achieved Rp16.6B — the highest among the three:contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.
  • Efficiency: 12/12 recorded the best budget-to-revenue ratio (1.71), making it the most cost-efficient.
  • Customer Engagement: 11/11 attracted the most customers (1,567), while 12/12 led in products sold (4,485).
  • Top Category: Men’s Fashion consistently contributed the highest revenue, transactions, and product counts across all campaigns.
  • A/B Testing Result: Group B outperformed Group A significantly (p-value ≈ 0.00), confirming that the new campaign design improved performance.

Recommendations

  • Combine Strengths: Integrate the wide reach of the 11/11 campaign (ads, messaging, timing) with 12/12’s efficiency in driving higher cart size through bundling or targeted promotions.
  • Category Focus: Prioritize Men’s Fashion in future promos as it demonstrates strong revenue and efficiency potential.
  • Campaign Design: Adopt Group B’s layout and mechanics company-wide for improved engagement rates.
  • Optimization: Reduce discount budget where possible, maintaining the 12/12 revenue-to-budget ratio as a benchmark.
