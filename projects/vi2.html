Telkom OCA — User Segmentation & Risk Monitoring

SQL-Based RFM Segmentation & Retention Strategy | RevoU x Telkom Indonesia

SQL RFM Segmentation Cohort Analysis Churn Risk

Background & Context

This project evaluated active user behavior on Telkom OCA’s messaging platform to understand engagement quality, churn risk, and revenue contribution across customers.

Approach, Method & Strategy

  • Performed SQL-based data preparation and feature engineering.
  • Applied rule-based RFM segmentation to classify 20 active users.
  • Mapped user segments to behavioral risk and engagement patterns.

Results & Key Insights

  • High-value users showed consistent usage and low churn risk.
  • Low-frequency users indicated early disengagement signals.
  • Distinct usage patterns emerged across segments.

Recommendations

  • Implement proactive engagement for low-frequency segments.
  • Design retention playbooks tailored to RFM groups.
  • Monitor at-risk users through periodic cohort reviews.
